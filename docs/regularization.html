Lasso and ridge

What does regularization do here?

Ridge (L2) shrinks every coefficient toward zero; lasso (L1) pushes some of them to exactly zero, switching sensors off. The strength C runs from 1e-6 (very strong) to 1 (weak). Watch performance and the coefficients change as C moves.
